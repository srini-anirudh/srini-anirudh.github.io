SciTech Council: building the AI and tech community

Epoch, the AI-ML and Data Science club, belongs to IIT Hyderabad’s SciTech Council. My work there ran through four threads that fed each other. Epoch taught the community and trained students. The Inter IIT Tech Meet tested that training against the other IITs, with Epoch’s cores on the problem teams. GDSC and Robotix were partners for workshops and projects.

Sessions & workshops

Open to the whole community. Each one opens as slides you can click through, and all of them are also on the talks page.

  • A Glimpse into the World of AIAug 13, 2022 · community

    A brief introduction to all the domains in AI: computer vision, NLP, reinforcement learning and more.

  • Introduction to ML ModelsAug 28, 2022 · community

    A brief introduction to modeling, a few basic terms and linear regression models, with a hands-on exercise on data collected from the audience.

  • Hello World: The AI ChapterNov 15, 2022 · freshers

    An introduction to the different domains of AI, for freshers.

  • Python for MLFeb 5, 2023 · community · with GDSC

    A hands-on session that builds on the Introduction to Python series by Lambda. It introduces NumPy and ends with a fun exercise on matrix transformations.

  • Programming for AIMar 18–19, 2023 · community · two-day workshop with GDSC

    The most important libraries: NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy and scikit-learn, with many hands-on exercises.

  • ML in AstronomyMar 22, 2023 · community · with Cepheid

    The statistics and ML libraries commonly used for astronomical data analysis, such as AstroML and AstroPy, with a few hands-on exercises.

  • Math for MLApr 1 and 4, 2023 · community · two-day workshop with GDSC

    The most important mathematical concepts: probability, statistics, linear algebra and calculus, with hands-on and theory exercises.

Projects

The projects from my term are collected in Projects-Consortium-2223. Two of them:

  • GesturesProEpoch, Robotix and Lambda

    Wearables such as smart rings and watches are mobile and talk to other devices wirelessly, so they could drive touchless interfaces. We train ML models on their accelerometer and gyroscope data to recognise gestures in real time: collect gestures, pre-process, extract features, recognise. We built an app that records the sensor data and streams it to a Python server over web sockets, plotted it live, and classified two gestures. Code.

  • Chess BotEpoch and Elektronika

    A bot that plays chess and moves the pieces with a robotic arm. Three phases: the code structure, the Pi camera and the chess engine; building the arm from servo motors; and tuning the arm to lift and place pieces accurately.
