Building Grounded Document Intelligence
Thesis talk · REPLICA, M3Grounder, DoCoG and DocLayout-VL

description

An hour-long talk that ties my document AI work together: a text representation of the page that loses nothing (REPLICA and FID-HTML), and grounding with a smart highlighter instead of typed coordinates: a VLM decides what to ground and SAM highlights it on the page as a mask, trained end to end (M3Grounder, DoCoG, DocLayout-VL), connected in a closed loop.

description
