ML Club sessions

Seven sessions and workshops I gave at IIT Hyderabad through the ML Club (Epoch, the AI-ML and Data Science Club), where I was head. They ran through 2022–23 and were open to the whole community.

A Glimpse into the World of AI
Community session · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · Aug 13, 2022

A brief introduction to all the domains in AI: computer vision, NLP, reinforcement learning and more.

Photos from the session
Introduction to ML Models
Community session · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · Aug 28, 2022

A brief introduction to modeling, a few basic terms and linear regression models, with a hands-on exercise on data collected from the audience.

Photos from the session
Hello World: The AI Chapter
Community session, for freshers · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · Nov 15, 2022

An introduction to the different domains of AI, for freshers.

Photos from the session
Python for ML
Community session, with GDSC · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · Feb 5, 2023

A hands-on session that builds on the Introduction to Python series by Lambda. It introduces NumPy and ends with a fun exercise on matrix transformations.

Photos from the session
Programming for AI
Two-day workshop, with GDSC · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · Mar 18–19, 2023

A hands-on workshop on the most important libraries: NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy and scikit-learn, with many exercises.

ML in Astronomy
Community session, with Cepheid · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · Mar 22, 2023

A hands-on session on the statistics and ML libraries commonly used for astronomical data analysis, such as AstroML and AstroPy, with a few exercises.

Photos from the session
Math for ML
Two-day workshop, with GDSC · Epoch, IIT Hyderabad · 1st April, 2023 and 4th April, 2023

A hands-on workshop on the most important mathematical concepts: probability, statistics, linear algebra and calculus, with hands-on and theory exercises.
