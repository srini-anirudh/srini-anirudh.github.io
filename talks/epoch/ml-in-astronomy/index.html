Central Limit Theorem o Given an arbitrary distribution h(x) characterized by its mean μ and standard deviation 𝞂, mean of N values xi drawn from that distribution will approximately follow a Gaussian distribution with the accuracy increasing with increasing N. o h(x) must have a standard deviation and therefore its tails must fall off faster than 1/x2 for large x o Central limit theorem can can be derived using method of characteristic functions (arXiv:0712.3028) or repeated convolutions (Gregory 2005). o Central limit theorem does not apply to Cauchy distributions, as it does not have a well-defined mean and standard deviation. o Sample Mean converges to distribution mean as sample size increases. This is called weak law of Large Numbers.
