Two document-specific losses

𝓛lmanswer text+ 𝓛segDice + BCE per mask+ 0.5 · 𝓛bleednew+ 0.5 · 𝓛hiernew

𝓛bleed · bleed suppression

SAM spills onto icons and graphics. Penalise every predicted mask pixel outside the ground-truth text.

𝓛hier · hierarchical enclosure

Phrase, line and block are three separate predictions. Penalise a finer mask outside its coarser one: phrase ⊂ line ⊂ block.

SFT cannot tell them apart

SFT checks one token at a time against one reference chain. Nothing scores a whole chain, so a right answer reached through wrong evidence is never penalised.

Preference training can

It compares two whole chains for the same question and pushes the model toward the better one.

Architecture

Same recipe as M3Grounder and DoCoG — except that the [LAY] hidden states now go through HGC (Hierarchical Grounding Contextualizer), which attends along the tree, before the segmenter.

Paper 3Architecture
Paper 3 · HGC

Why attention should follow the tree

Each [LAY] attends only to its parent, children and siblings — the coloured blocks in the mask.

So a headline stays with its article, an item with its menu section — regions of different branches never merge.

Paper 3 · Training objective

Structure-aware losses

𝓛lmthe tagged tree+ 2 · 𝓛segDice + BCE per mask+ 0.5 · 𝓛structnew+ 0.5 · 𝓛bgnew

𝓛struct · the masks must respect the tree

Containment: a child’s mask outside its parent is penalised. Overlap: two siblings claiming the same pixels are penalised.

𝓛bg · masks stay off the background

Every predicted pixel outside the page’s ground-truth foreground — whitespace and background — is penalised.

M3Grounder’s two priors, now on a tree: enclosure → containment, bleed → background.
