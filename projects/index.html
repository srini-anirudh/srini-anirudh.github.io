IIT Hyderabad Projects

My undergraduate research and club projects at IIT Hyderabad, grouped by interest. My work at BharatGen, including Patram and e-vikrAI, is on the Research page.

Speech Processing

ROBOVOX: Far-field Speaker Recognition by a Mobile Robot
Winner, IEEE Signal Processing Cup 2024. Speech Lab, IIT Hyderabad. Mentored by Dr. Sri Rama Murty Kodukula.

description
  • Worked on speaker authentication under ambient noise, robot noise, reverberation, variable distance, babble noise, and diverse speaker-microphone angles.
  • Won the IEEE Signal Processing Cup 2024 and received the $5000 winner prize.
  • Built the baseline using score fusion of SEResNet and ECAPA-TDNN on multi-channel data.
  • Achieved minDCF 0.7096 on the ROBOVOX dataset, with ensembling lowering EER to 9.59%.

Quantum Computing

Quantum Spherical K-Means Clustering
Mentored by Dr. M.V. Panduranga Rao.

description
  • Implemented Spherical q-Means from scratch using Qiskit.
  • Applied it to Multi-Depot Vehicle Routing Problem with angular clustering considerations.
  • Achieved a Davies-Bouldin Index of 0.742 and improved total routing cost across 13 MD-VRP datasets compared to q-Means and Spherical K-Means.
Quantum Computing Approaches to Vehicle Routing Problem
Mentored by Dr. M.V. Panduranga Rao.

description
  • Surveyed classical and quantum methods for the vehicle routing problem and its variants.
  • Focused on Multiple Vehicle Visits for flexible and efficient routing.
  • Compared LP runtime in CPLEX with CQM QPU access time in D-Wave, showing superior CQM performance.
  • Explored QUBO formulations and additional routing variants.
MPhasis: Quantum Computing Challenge
Inter IIT Tech Meet 12.0.

description
  • Built a D-Wave-based business rules engine using Discrete Quadratic Models for automated assessment of airline schedule changes.
  • Optimized alternate flight selection based on travel time and ancillary service impact.
  • Generated default and exception solution files for passengers using industry data models and rules.
Quantum Random Walks
Mentored by Dr. M.V. Panduranga Rao.

description
  • Studied quantum random walks as the quantum counterpart of Markov chains: coined walks on a line and on regular graphs, with Grover’s diffusion coin.
  • Compared absorption, mixing and hitting times with classical walks, and covered Szegedy walks.
  • Explored walks on directed and weighted graphs and a simple model-checking idea.

Explainable AI

XAI for Forecasting in Climate Sciences: Disturbance Storm Time (DST) Index
IIT Hyderabad. Mentored by Dr. Shruti Upadhyaya.

description
  • Forecast the Disturbance Storm Time (Dst) index, a measure of geomagnetic storms, from NOAA DSCOVR and NASA ACE solar-wind data.

Convolutions: The Big Picture
IIT Hyderabad. Mentored by Dr. Mohd Suhail Rizvi.

description
  • Argues that convolutions in probability, signal processing, statistical mechanics and information theory are one structure, so theorems can be carried between fields.
  • Proves the Central Limit Theorem two ways and reads it as a fixed point, a maximum-entropy attractor and an equilibrium argument.
  • Works through six cross-field transfers, including Stein’s lemma and Price’s theorem, and De Bruijn’s identity and the heat equation.
